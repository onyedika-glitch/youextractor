Connect

Time to open the connection. Keep it short.

connectTimeout(5)

Read / idle

Time between bytes. Resets when data arrives, so a slow trickle never trips it.

timeout=(3.05, 27)

Total deadline

The whole request, start to finish, no matter what. Often the one that's missing.

Job / worker

The whole unit of work. The last line of defense around everything inside.
