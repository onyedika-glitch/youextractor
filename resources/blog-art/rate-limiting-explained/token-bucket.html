Token bucket

Two settings: burst size and refill rate.

Full

A user opens a page and fires 6 requests at once. All pass.

Quiet

Nothing for a minute. Tokens drip back in until the bucket is full again.

Script

Nonstop requests empty the bucket. After that, only 1 every 6 seconds gets through.

Settings

Bucket size = biggest burst you allow. Refill rate = long-term average.
