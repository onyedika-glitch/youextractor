A 429 that clients can actually use

Say what happened, when to come back, and how much room is left.

HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests
Retry-After: 40
X-RateLimit-Limit: 3
X-RateLimit-Remaining: 0
Content-Type: application/json

{ "error": "You can start 3 extractions
  per minute. Try again in 40 seconds." }
1
429, not 503. 503 looks like your server is down.
2
Retry-After in seconds. Good clients wait exactly this long.
3
Limit and remaining, so clients slow down before they hit the wall.
4
A human message your UI can show with a countdown.
