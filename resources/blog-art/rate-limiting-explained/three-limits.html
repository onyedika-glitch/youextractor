Three limits that get called "rate limit"

They answer different questions, so you often need more than one.

How fast?

Rate limit

Requests per second, minute or day. Smooths bursts, stops scripts.

5 per minute
How many?

Quota

A total over a long period or a plan. About money, not speed.

1/1 used
1 free extraction
How many at once?

Concurrency

Work running at the same time. Protects slow, expensive jobs.

2 jobs at once
