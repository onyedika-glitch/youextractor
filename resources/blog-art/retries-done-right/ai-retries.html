Two kinds of AI failure, two kinds of retry

A broken connection and a broken answer need different handling.

Transport failure

The call didn't work

504 Gateway Timeout · 429 · 503
  1. Back off with jitter, respect Retry-After
  2. Cap attempts inside the time budget
  3. Fall back to another provider
Bad answer

The call worked, the output didn't

Unexpected token '`' · missing "files"
  1. Clean up first: strip fences, parse again
  2. Retry with feedback: send it back, ask to fix only the format
  3. Stop after 1–2 tries, then fall back or fail clearly
